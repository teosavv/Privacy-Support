# Privacy Policy — Velvet Haptics

**Last Updated:** August 21, 2026

**Summary:** Velvet Haptics collects ZERO personal data. We do not track you, we do not store server logs, and we do not sell your information. Your intimate sessions and remote touch connections are 100% private and end-to-end encrypted.

---

## 1. Zero Data Collection
Velvet Haptics is designed from the ground up as a privacy-first application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII). All haptic pattern recordings, settings, and usage preferences remain strictly stored on your local device.

## 2. Real-Time Remote Couple Touch Privacy
When using our Partner Sync (Remote Couple Touch) feature, live touch signals are transmitted directly between devices using peer-to-peer (P2P) WebRTC protocols secured with end-to-end AES-GCM encryption. No audio, video, or touch data is recorded, saved, or routed through central cloud storage servers.

## 3. Local Biometric Protection
If Face ID or Touch ID protection is enabled, authentication is processed entirely locally by iOS via Apple's Secure Enclave. Velvet Haptics never accesses or stores your biometric credentials.

## 4. Third-Party Analytics & Tracking
Velvet Haptics contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, zero advertising networks, and zero tracking frameworks. We do not track your activity across other apps or websites.

## 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: **support@velvethaptics.com**.
