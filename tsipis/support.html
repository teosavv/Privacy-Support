Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I track a product?

There are two easy ways:

  • In Safari: Tap the Share button while viewing any online store page and tap TSIPIS. The product details are imported with one tap.
  • In the App: Tap the + button in the top right corner and paste the product link.

How often are prices checked?

TSIPIS periodically monitors prices in the cloud. You can also manually pull-to-refresh on the main dashboard to check prices immediately.

How do price alerts work?

Open any tracked product, tap the target price threshold to set your desired price. When the product price drops to or below your target, you will receive an instant notification.

Does TSIPIS require an account?

No. TSIPIS uses an anonymous, privacy-first architecture. All your tracked items remain private on your personal devices.

Which platforms are supported?

TSIPIS supports iPhone (all screen sizes including Dynamic Island and foldable displays), iPad (fluid 3-pill grid), Apple Watch (standalone watchOS companion), and Home Screen Widgets.
