# Pocket QTH - Support & Help Center

Welcome to the **Pocket QTH** support center. Pocket QTH is a mobile location and Maidenhead Grid Square utility engineered for amateur radio operators, field day activations, SOTA/POTA operators, and DXers.

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How does Pocket QTH calculate my Maidenhead Grid Square?
Pocket QTH uses your device's GPS hardware via Apple Core Location to receive your exact latitude and longitude. It runs an offline algorithm locally on your device to map coordinates to standard 4-character and 6-character Maidenhead locators (e.g., `JO21vw`).

### 2. Does Pocket QTH work without cellular service or internet?
**Yes, 100%!** All Maidenhead locator math, coordinate conversions, altitude calculations, and antenna bearing formulas work completely offline using onboard GPS hardware.

### 3. How do I enable Lock Screen Live Activities & Dynamic Island?
To show your Maidenhead Grid Square on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island:
1. Tap the **`SHOW WIDGET`** pill button in the top-right header card.
2. Ensure **Live Activities** are enabled in **iOS Settings ➔ Pocket QTH ➔ Live Activities**.

### 4. How does iCloud Sync work for saved bookmarks?
When you save a QTH location or station bookmark, Pocket QTH saves it via SwiftData. If you are signed into iCloud on your iOS devices, Apple CloudKit automatically syncs your bookmarks across your devices in the background.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting & Diagnostics

- **Location Not Updating:** Ensure location permissions are set to *"While Using the App"* or *"Always"* in **iOS Settings ➔ Privacy & Security ➔ Location Services ➔ Pocket QTH**.
- **Distance Unit Toggle:** You can switch between Kilometers (`km`) and Miles (`mi`) at any time using the segment control on the main dashboard.
