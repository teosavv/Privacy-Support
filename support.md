## Frequently Asked Questions

### 1. How does Real-Time Remote Couple Touch work?
Open the Partner Sync menu inside Velvet Haptics, generate a pairing PIN or QR code, and send it to your partner. Once connected, touch pulses recorded on your screen stream in real-time to your partner's iPhone with sub-50ms latency.

### 2. Why don't I feel vibrations when the phone is on Silent mode?
Ensure **System Haptics** is enabled in **iOS Settings > Sounds & Haptics**. Velvet Haptics uses Apple's hardware Taptic Engine which operates independently of system volume.

### 3. How do I close the Dynamic Island or Lock Screen Widget?
You can terminate the active session directly from your Lock Screen or Dynamic Island by tapping the quick close **(X)** button on the widget, or by stopping the active rhythm inside the main app.

### 4. Are there any monthly subscription fees?
No! Velvet Haptics is a one-time purchase. You receive 100% unlocked access to all features, custom rhythm studio, and partner sync forever with zero subscriptions.
